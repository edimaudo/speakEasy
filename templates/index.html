Settings & privacy

Audio

Microphone audio is processed in your browser. SpeakEasy does not upload or store audio files.

Transcript

Your transcript is sent to Firebase AI Logic for coaching. Completed sessions are saved only in this browser for up to 30 days.

History

History and the daily practice allowance are stored locally in this browser using IndexedDB. They are not uploaded to SpeakEasy servers or synced between devices. Clearing this site's browser data removes them.

AI processing

Prompt generation prefers on-device Gemini Nano. Coaching prefers cloud Gemini and can fall back according to Firebase AI Logic's hybrid mode.
